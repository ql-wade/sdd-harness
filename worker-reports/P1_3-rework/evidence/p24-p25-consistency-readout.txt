                          P1-03 dispatch.generation must equal currentGeneration;

[4] Isomorphism verdict per semantic
  role:      isomorphic. Both sides make role a launcher/runtime-ATTESTED property
             the worker cannot mint, and both refuse builder as acceptance signer
             (0002: role == GATE_ROLES[gate] at pass evidence; local: builder ->
             IDENTITY_CONFLICT, unknown -> ROLE_NOT_DISPATCHED). Nuance (not a
             conflict): 0002 derives role from the gate id at issue; the local
             launcher fixes role at issue from {verifier, test-author, builder} and
             constrains at admission. Direction identical.
  inputDigest: isomorphic. One-shot binding on both sides; changed inputs refuse
             acceptance (0002 D02 <-> STALE_CANDIDATE / STALE_AUTHORIZATION /
             ARTIFACT_DIGEST_MISMATCH).
  generation: consistent with division of labor; no contradiction found.
             0002 is the generation ENFORCER (persisted monotonic registry). The
             local face carries the same currency binding minus the generation
             field: its binding carries epoch, and currency is judged on
             inputDigest/policyDigest/environment against caller-held current state.
             A same-digest byte-restore can therefore revive an OLD dispatch only in
             the local admit path — the exact case 0002 closes in the runtime — and
             the local face has no persisted invalidation registry to judge against.
             This is the documented local-equivalent limitation of
             lib/delivery/evidence.js ("collaboration-grade checks locally, not
             tamper resistance"); the adapter never dispatches gates itself (the
             single runtime dispatches, Comet.Run.managedDelivery.v1), so no P1_3
             behavior depends on local generation attestation. ROUTE (recorded, not
             actioned here): if local generation attestation is ever required, it
             belongs in lib/delivery/evidence.js (P2_2 owns), not in the P1_3 face.
  Related seam checked: 0003 keeps a managed run open while the knowledge closeout
  is pending (H07); P1_3's sealCloseout ordering (Kp before seal) and its refusal to
  re-seal agree with that runtime behavior (observed in the unmanaged probe lane).
